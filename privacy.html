Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how Avi Avigav ("we") handles data when you use Field Proof, an app for monday.com ("the app"). Contact: avi4motion@gmail.com.

Summary

Field Proof runs inside monday.com. It has no servers or databases of its own. Reports you send are written to your own monday.com board. We do not see, collect, sell or share your report data.

Data on your device

Reports waiting to be sent (including photos) are kept in the browser storage (IndexedDB) of the device that created them, and are deleted from the device once sent. A copy of the board setup is cached in the device's local storage. Removing the app or clearing the monday app's data removes them.

Access to your monday.com account

The app uses monday.com's own authentication and requests only these permissions: read your user profile (me:read), read boards (boards:read), write to boards (boards:write) to create items, columns and files, and post updates (updates:write). It acts only on the board where you open it.

Third parties

The app is hosted by monday.com (monday code, *.monday.app) and talks only to monday.com APIs. The update on each item contains a link to Google Maps for the report location; Google receives data only if you click it. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Your reports live on your monday.com board and are controlled by you under monday.com's terms. We keep no copy. If you uninstall the app, it stops accessing your account; nothing needs to be deleted on our side. Data on a device can be removed by deleting waiting reports in the Outbox or clearing the app data.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher) between your device and monday.com. We do not store access tokens.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, requests to access or delete report data are handled in monday.com. For any question or request, email avi4motion@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes

We will update this page if the app starts processing data differently, and change the date above.
