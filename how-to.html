How to use Field Proof

From install to the first report takes about five minutes.

Before you start

  • A monday.com board where the reports should land (a new or existing board).
  • Board owner or admin rights for the one-time setup. Team members only need access to the board.
  • For field staff: the monday.com mobile app on Android or iPhone.

Set up (once per board)

  1. Add the view. Open your board, click + next to the board views, choose Apps and add Field Proof.
  2. Pick your templates. Open the Setup tab and tick the reports your team fills, for example In-store display installation and Store visit. Click Save & create columns. Field Proof adds the matching columns and reuses columns that already exist with the same name.

Send a report (field staff)

  1. Open the board on the phone and switch to the Field Proof view.
  2. Choose the report and fill in the fields. Location is captured automatically when the form opens.
  3. Add photos with Camera or Gallery. Photos are compressed on the phone to save data.
  4. Get a signature (on templates that have one). The on-site manager signs with a finger; Undo removes the last stroke.
  5. Tap Submit report. A new item is created with the answers in their columns, an update with the full report, and the photos, signature and PDF in the files column.

No signal?

Submit anyway. The report waits in the Outbox tab and is sent automatically when the phone is back online. You can also tap Retry.
